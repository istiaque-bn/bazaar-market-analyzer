Product documentation

A practical guide to Stock Bazaar's current features and their limitations.

Market research

Use Stocks, the market screener, sector heatmap and comparison tools to explore available securities and calculated research signals. The footer shows the most recent successful data-update time.

Portfolio and investor journal

Create a portfolio, add holdings or transactions manually, or import a broker-style CSV. For each investment, you can record a thesis, target, risk/invalidation and post-trade review. Your portfolio remains a private record; it does not connect to or place orders with a broker.

Watchlists and alerts

Add stocks to your watchlist and create the alerts relevant to your research. Notifications are informational and can be delayed or unavailable.

Language and appearance

Choose English or বাংলা from the navigation menu. Use the Dark/Light control in the same menu to select a colour theme; the choice is saved only in your browser.

Research limits

Signals, forecasts, models and backtests are educational estimates, not trade instructions. They can be wrong or lose effectiveness. Review the risk, methodology and data notices before acting on any information.
