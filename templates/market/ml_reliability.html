ML Reliability Monitor

Statistical evaluation of settled predictions vs. rule-based and naive baselines — staff-only

Every status below reflects only what settled predictions have actually shown so far — "insufficient evidence" and "no demonstrated edge" are real, common outcomes here, not errors. Nothing on this page is a claim that a model is "safe" or "profitable" — see the economic diagnostics on each card for why. Run python manage.py assess_ml_reliability --json for the full structured output, and docs/RUNBOOKS.md for how to investigate and act on any non-healthy status.
