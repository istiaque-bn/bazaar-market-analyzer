## Product summary

Stock Bazaar is a web-based educational market-analysis and stock-monitoring product. Users can review market information, technical calculations, research estimates, watchlists and private portfolio/journal records. The product does not accept or execute orders, custody assets, provide brokerage services or provide personalised investment advice.

## Questions for BSEC/legal review

1. Does the proposed feature set require any registration, approval or licence under Bangladesh securities laws or regulations?
2. Are the current user-facing disclosures, terms, risk warning, conflicts disclosure, privacy policy and model-methodology disclosure adequate?
3. What controls, records, approvals or marketing restrictions are required before public launch?
4. Which features or wording must change before paid plans, financial promotions, model forecasts or alerts are offered?

## Questions for DSE/CSE/data-provider review

1. Which data products, licences and attribution are required for the data currently displayed or stored?
2. Is delayed display permitted, and what delay, attribution and redistribution conditions apply?
3. Is use of data for screening, analytics, model training, alerts, email/Telegram digests or backtests permitted?
4. What licences are required before a commercial service, real-time display, API access or user redistribution?

## Current controls and disclosures

- Public pages: Privacy Policy, Terms, Risk Disclosure, Conflict-of-Interest Disclosure, Model Methodology, Refund Policy and Market Data & Licensing Notice.
- Product wording says analysis is educational; it is not brokerage, execution, custody or personalised advice.
- Model outputs are monitored against simpler baselines and may be limited or disabled where evidence is weak.
- Data timestamp is shown to authenticated users.
- Daily encrypted database/model backups are verified structurally, retained locally in a rotation and copied to encrypted Google Drive.
